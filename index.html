01 · Competitor Intelligence · Self-built
Hawkeye - Competitor Price & Promotion Monitor
Tracks what competitors do on their sites: price changes, new promotions and product launches, in one live dashboard with alerts. Every competitor site is built differently (platform, anti-bot protection, data hidden in JSON or plain HTML), so each gets its own custom adapter and the data is normalised into one format. Runs 24/7 or on any schedule, every few hours or daily, with a wider or narrower scope per competitor.

Product Builder - GTIN to Shopify-ready product
Builds a product from a single GTIN. Finds source content across countries (often Dutch, Swiss, Australian or NZ sites when a product isn't live in Ireland), flags each match green or yellow for review, builds to the store's category style, and lets you override the reference product.
